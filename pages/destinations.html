Rameswaram

Rameswaram is located on a beautiful island in the lower side of India, in the Gulf of Mannar and the Palk Strait. Lord Shiva is worshipped here and it is considered one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites in Hinduism.

The Ramanathaswamy Temple is famous for its magnificent corridors — the longest temple corridors in the world. The Pamban Bridge connecting Rameswaram to mainland India is an engineering marvel worth seeing. Dhanushkodi, the ghost town at the island's tip, is a surreal experience.

Kanyakumari

Kanyakumari is the southernmost tip of India where three seas meet — the Arabian Sea, the Bay of Bengal, and the Indian Ocean. Surrounded by majestic hills and plains bordered by colourful sea-shores, fringed with coconut trees and paddy fields.

It is famous for the stunning sunrise and sunset views. The Vivekananda Rock Memorial, Thiruvalluvar Statue, Kumari Amman Temple and the Gandhi Memorial are must-visit attractions. The multicoloured sand on the beaches is a unique natural marvel.

Sunrise & Sunset Vivekananda Rock Thiruvalluvar Statue Kumari Amman Temple Gandhi Memorial Wax Museum

Madurai

Madurai is a major city and cultural headquarters of Tamil Nadu. Known as the "City of Festivals," it is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world with a history spanning over 2,500 years.

The Meenakshi Amman Temple is a masterpiece of Dravidian architecture with towering gopurams adorned with thousands of colourful sculptures. Madurai is also famous for its jasmine flowers, cotton sarees, and mouth-watering cuisine.

Meenakshi Amman Temple Thirumalai Nayak Palace Gandhi Museum Alagar Koil Vaigai River Koodal Azhagar Temple

Ooty (Udhagamandalam)

Ooty, the Queen of Hill Stations, is set at an altitude of 2,240 metres in the Nilgiri Hills. It is a town and municipality in Tamil Nadu, serving as the district headquarters of the Nilgiris district.

The scenic Toy Train ride from Mettupalayam to Ooty through lush greenery is a UNESCO World Heritage experience. Ooty Lake, Botanical Gardens, Doddabetta Peak, and Avalanche Lake make it one of the most popular getaways from South India.

Toy Train (UNESCO) Ooty Lake Botanical Gardens Doddabetta Peak Avalanche Lake Rose Garden

Kodaikanal

Kodaikanal is a city in the hills of Dindigul district in Tamil Nadu. Its name in Tamil means "Gift of the Forest." Situated at an altitude of 2,133 metres above sea level, it is called the Princess of Hill Stations.

The star-shaped Kodaikanal Lake is the most popular tourist spot, ideal for boating and cycling. Coaker's Walk, Pillar Rocks, Bear Shola Falls, and Green Valley Views are must-see attractions. The Kurinji flower blooms here every 12 years, painting the hills violet.

Kerala

Kerala, on the tropical Malabar Coast of India, is one of the most popular tourist destinations in Asia. Known as "God's Own Country," it is famous for its palm-lined beaches, backwaters, tea estates, and Ayurveda.

Munnar's tea gardens, Alleppey's houseboat backwaters, Thekkady's wildlife, Kovalam's beaches, and Thiruvananthapuram's cultural heritage all combine to make Kerala an unforgettable experience. We offer complete Kerala tour packages from Rameswaram.

Thanjavur

Thanjavur, also known as Tanjore, is a temple city in Tamil Nadu and is considered one of the most important cultural centres of South India. It was the capital of the Chola Dynasty, one of the greatest empires in Indian history.

The Brihadeeswarar Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site built in the 11th century, is one of the greatest masterpieces of Tamil architecture. The 66-metre tall vimana (tower) casts no shadow at noon. Thanjavur is also famous for its classical Bharatanatyam dance and Tanjore paintings.

Brihadeeswarar Temple (UNESCO) Royal Palace Museum Saraswathi Mahal Library Sivaganga Park Tanjore Paintings Chola Bronze Art

Tiruchirappalli (Trichy)

Trichy is the fourth largest city of Tamil Nadu and a major transportation hub of the state. Located on the banks of River Kaveri, it has a rich history going back to the Sangam period.

The famous Rock Fort Temple sits atop a 83-metre high rock and offers panoramic views of the city. The Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple in Srirangam — one of the largest functioning Hindu temples in the world — covers 156 acres and has 21 gopurams. Trichy is a must-visit on any South India pilgrimage circuit.

Rock Fort Temple Ranganathaswamy Temple Jambukeswarar Temple Kaveri River Srirangam Island Lourde's Church

Chennai

Chennai, the capital city of Tamil Nadu, is a major cultural, economic, and educational hub of South India. Known as the "Detroit of India" for its automobile industry, it is also a city of great temples, beaches, and heritage.

Marina Beach — the world's second longest urban beach — is a must-visit. The Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Government Museum, Fort St. George, and Santhome Cathedral are key attractions. We offer reliable taxi services from Rameswaram to Chennai airport, railway station, and hotels.
