Top Sights in Rameswaram

Ramanathaswamy Temple

One of India's 12 Jyotirlinga shrines, famous for its 1,212-metre-long corridors — the longest temple corridors in the world.

Dhanushkodi

The ghost town at the island's southeastern tip, where the Bay of Bengal meets the Indian Ocean. Surreal and hauntingly beautiful.

Pamban Bridge

India's first sea bridge and second longest. The railway bridge opens to let ships pass — a spectacular engineering wonder.

Agni Theertham

The sacred sea beach right in front of the Ramanathaswamy Temple. Pilgrims take a holy dip here before entering the temple.

Kothandaramaswamy Temple

An ancient temple on the seashore near Dhanushkodi, said to be where Vibhishana surrendered to Lord Rama.

Gandhamadhana Parvatham

The highest point on Rameswaram island, with a shrine housing Lord Rama's footprints and a panoramic view of the island.

Panchmukhi Hanuman Temple

A unique temple with a five-faced Hanuman idol believed to be the spot where Hanuman rested after bringing the Sanjeevani herb.

Adam's Bridge (Ram Setu)

The legendary chain of limestone shoals connecting Rameswaram to Mannar Island, Sri Lanka — the mythical bridge built by Rama's army.

Visitor Tips

🕐 Best Time to Visit

October to April is ideal. Avoid peak summer (May–July). Early morning temple visits (5–7 AM) are peaceful and less crowded.

👕 Dress Code

Traditional attire is recommended at Ramanathaswamy Temple. Men must remove shirts before entering the inner sanctum. Carry a spare dhoti.

🌊 22 Sacred Theerthams

Pilgrims bathe in 22 sacred wells inside the temple. Each has a different taste and healing property. Carry a change of clothes.

🚗 Dhanushkodi Access

The last 18 km to Dhanushkodi tip is sandy beach road — only 4WD beach jeeps can access it. Our packages include jeep transfer arrangements.

📷 Photography

Photography is not permitted inside the Ramanathaswamy Temple. Cameras must be deposited at the entrance. Mobile cameras are usually allowed.

🏨 Stay & Food

Rameswaram has many good vegetarian hotels and dharamshalas. We can arrange hotel bookings — contact us while booking your taxi package.
