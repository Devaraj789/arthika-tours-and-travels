Who We Are

Arthika Tours and Travels is Rameswaram's No.1 taxi and tour service provider. With over 8 years of experience, we have built a reputation for safe, reliable, and affordable travel across South India.

We are based in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, and serve thousands of pilgrims, families, and corporate travellers each year. Our fleet includes well-maintained AC vehicles — from Sedans and Innova Crysta to Tempo Travellers and Mini Buses — driven by experienced, licensed, and courteous drivers.

Whether you're planning a pilgrimage to the Ramanathaswamy Temple, a family holiday to Kerala, or a business trip to Madurai, Arthika Tours has the perfect vehicle and package for you.
